**La vie privée des volcans** (*The Private Life of Volcanoes*) — a scientific and musical experience, presented as part of **CLEEDI 2026**.

{{< img "images/flyer_concert_cleedi_2026_recto.jpg" "Concert poster — La vie privée des volcans, 10 November 2026">}}

## When
Tuesday **10 November 2026** — doors open 18:15, concert 18:45–20:30.

## Where
Ongaku-no-Tomo Hall, 6-30 Kagurazaka, Shinjuku-ku, Tokyo 162-8716. [Access map](https://www.ontomo-hall.ongakunotomo.co.jp/access/)

## Program
- Ludwig van Beethoven — Octet, Op. 103
- *Sismologie : la musique de la terre pour Jean-Paul Montagner* (2024) — Nobuaki Fuji
- *La vie privée des volcans* (2026, world premiere) — Nobuaki Fuji, dedicated to Fidel Costa

Performed by **PhiloGaïa Tokyo**, conducted by Kenya Masakado. With remarks from Jean-Paul Montagner and Fidel Costa, hosted and interpreted by Yosuke Aoki (Earthquake Research Institute, The University of Tokyo).

## Organized with
Funded entirely by the MuseSeLFiE project (Institut Universitaire de France & IPGP), with support from the Earthquake Research Institute, The University of Tokyo (celebrating its 100th anniversary). Production: Salon d'art.

## Admission
Free entry. Reservation preferred but not required, via [teket](https://teket.jp) — booking is expected to open the week of 22 September 2026 (link will be added here once live).
