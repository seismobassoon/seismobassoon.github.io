03/2013-06/2013, M1, Université Pierre-et-Marie-Curie, supervised by Lapo Boschi, Laëtitia Le Pourhiet and me at UPMC. Coming from the physics department, he worked on the resolution of surface waves. We tested seismic interferometry with synthetic data. He then did his PhD with Jean-Paul Montagner and Martin Vallée on prompt elasto-gravity signals related to large earthquakes. He is a postdoc in Nantes.
