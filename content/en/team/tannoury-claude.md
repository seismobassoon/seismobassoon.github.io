01/2015-06/2015, M2R, supervised by Éléonore Stutzmann, Jean-Paul Montagner and me. We wanted to extract Green's functions from cross-correlations of seismic noise recorded in Greenland. Claude, a serious student, worked a lot with Éléonore, but time ran out and we could not model synthetic seismograms. On the other hand, we exchanged a lot during her internship and she deepened her understanding of theoretical seismology. After a PhD in physics in Rennes, she now teaches at Efrei Paris, the "Grande École du numérique".
